# UTSW-Glioma BT0156 Attribution

The 30 image files in `images/` are selected 2D PNG derivatives of subject
BT0156 from the UTSW-Glioma collection. They were modified from the source
NIfTI volumes by selecting individual axial slices and exporting them as PNG.
No diagnostic labels or generated predictions are included.

Data citation:

Reddy, D., Saadat, N., Holcomb, J., Wagner, B., Truong, N., Bowerman, J.,
Hatanpaa, K., Patel, T., Pinho, M., Yu, F., Zhang, K., Lodhi, S.,
Madhuranthakam, A., Bangalore Yogananda, C. G., & Maldjian, J. (2026). The
University of Texas Southwestern Glioma MRI dataset with molecular marker
characterization and segmentations (UTSW-Glioma) (Version 1) [Data set]. The
Cancer Imaging Archive. https://doi.org/10.7937/DFAE-1B86
